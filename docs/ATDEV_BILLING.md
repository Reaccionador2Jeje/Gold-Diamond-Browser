# AtDev Billing — Hybrid plans (Gold & Diamond Browser)

**Status:** proposal only. The browser's essential browsing functionality remains free.

## Hybrid model

Keep normal browsing, privacy controls, security updates, and core settings free. Do not introduce paid tracking, sell browsing history, or weaken privacy for free users. Optional monetization may include voluntary support and cosmetic customization that does not affect safety or privacy.

## Proposed tiers (CLP/month; exploratory)

| Plan | Reference price | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Free | $0 | Full essential browser functionality |
| Supporter | Voluntary | Optional contribution and supporter acknowledgment |
| Customization Plus | $490 | Future cosmetic themes and interface personalization, if implemented |

No checkout or paid feature is active. Browser telemetry must remain opt-in, minimal, and documented. Never collect browsing history for advertising or monetization. Any real-money launch requires adult account-owner review of provider eligibility and legal/tax obligations.
